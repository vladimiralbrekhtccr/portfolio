Selected work 2024 – 2026

Featured · first author

FogGen: a self-aware edge–cloud LLM router

A 0.6B model on the phone writes a confidence score before it answers. When it is not sure, it hands the question to a 30B model in the cloud. I built the closed loop of SFT and conditional GRPO that trains this behaviour over 14 rounds in seven domains.
